I am a postdoctoral scholar at New York University, working in the group of Prof. [Aditi Mitra](https://as.nyu.edu/faculty/aditi-mitra.html). I completed my PhD in physics at Rutgers University, New Brunswick, advised by Prof. [Ananda Roy](https://sites.rutgers.edu/ananda-roy/), and before that studied at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune, and the Australian National University (ANU).


<!--click [here](assets/pdf/CV_Madhav_Sinha.pdf) for my CV.-->

My research focuses on generalized symmetries and topological defects in condensed matter systems, both in and out of equilibrium. A full list of publications is available on [Inspire](https://inspirehep.net/authors/2718620).
